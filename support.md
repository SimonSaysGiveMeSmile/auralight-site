# AuraLight — Support

AuraLight controls Bluetooth (ELK/BLEDOM‑family) LED lighting controllers in your car — colors, brightness, animated effects, and music‑reactive lighting.

## Getting started

1. Power your LED controller and make sure it isn't connected in another app.
2. Open AuraLight — it scans automatically. Tap your controller in the list to connect.
3. Confirm your strip count when prompted (this keeps brightness within a safe range).
4. Use the tabs: **Static** (solid colors), **Animated** (effects), **Music** (react to audio), **Setup** (connection, layout, preferences).

No controller yet? Tap **Demo Controller** on the connect screen to explore the whole app without hardware.

## Common questions

- **It won't find my controller.** Make sure Bluetooth is on, the controller is powered, and it isn't already connected in its original app (only one app can hold the connection). Pull to rescan, or use "show all devices."
- **The lights don't react to music.** Music‑reactive effects use the phone's microphone to *hear* the cabin audio, so the music must be audibly playing near the phone. Grant microphone access when prompted (Settings → AuraLight).
- **Warp Drive (experimental).** This driving‑dynamics effect is off by default; enable it in **Setup**. It's intended for passenger use.
