What it does

Built for the driver's seat.

Most LED controllers ship with clunky apps and abrupt effects. AuraLight is glanceable, precise, and computed on your phone for a fluidity the hardware can't do on its own.

01 / Color & brightness

A full spectrum, dimmed without the staircase.

One-tap colors and an exact picker, with high-resolution brightness that glides instead of stepping — a dual-dimmer path tuned in perceptual light space, so the fade stays smooth all the way down.

02 / Animated effects

Breathe, flow, and ambient scenes.

Breathe, Color Flow, and a Rainbow that runs through only the colors you choose — plus ambient scenes like Fireplace, Ocean, Aurora, Lava and Thunderstorm, and one-tap moods tuned for the moment.

  • Phone-rendered for fluidity the controller can't reach alone
  • Cruise · Sunset · City · Canyon quick moods
03 / Music-reactive

Lights that lock to the beat — zero lag.

Play a track right in the app and AuraLight drives the lights straight from its sound, perfectly in time and with no microphone needed. Or let the mic-based effects move your lights with the cabin audio.

  • In-app playback for tight, zero-lag reactivity
  • Sound Wave · Beat Breathe · Style Sync via the mic
  • Analyzed live, on-device — audio is never recorded or sent
04 / Built for the car

Two looks. Hands-free. Remembers everything.

Switch between a precise instrument-cluster look and a living-color Aurora, in light or dark mode. Control it hands-free with Siri, and AuraLight reconnects to your controller automatically and resumes right where you left off.

  • “Set AuraLight to Night.” · “Turn AuraLight on.”
  • Full VoiceOver, Dynamic Type & Reduce Motion support
  • Universal — iPhone & iPad
Data Not Collected

Nothing leaves your phone.

No account. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. AuraLight makes no network connections at all — your scenes, controller, and settings live only on your device. Microphone and motion are analyzed live, in memory, and never recorded or sent anywhere.

No network calls No third-party SDKs On-device only No login
