What it does

A look for every mood.

Start with your favorite color, find the right brightness and save the combination for next time. Designed with car interiors in mind — set up your lighting while parked.

01 / Color & brightness

A full spectrum, dimmed without the staircase.

Choose a quick swatch or find your own color with the picker. Adjust the brightness, then save a scene so your favorite look is always a tap away. Dimming behavior depends on your controller.

02 / Animated effects

Breathe, flow, and ambient scenes.

Breathe, Color Flow, and a Rainbow that runs through only the colors you choose — plus ambient scenes like Fireplace, Ocean, Aurora, Lava and Thunderstorm, and one-tap moods tuned for the moment.

  • Animated colors rendered on your phone
  • Cruise · Sunset · City · Canyon quick moods
03 / Music-reactive

Give your music a little color.

Choose an audio file to play in the app and let your lights react to its sound without using the microphone. Or use microphone-based effects with music playing nearby. Response timing varies with your Bluetooth controller.

  • In-app audio playback with reactive lighting
  • Sound Wave · Beat Breathe · Style Sync via the mic
  • Analyzed live, on-device — audio is never recorded or sent
04 / Built for the car

Your favorite look, ready to return.

Switch between Instrument and Aurora styles, in light or dark mode. Recall scenes with Siri or Shortcuts and reconnect to your remembered controller. Bluetooth control requires a compatible connection; you may need to open AuraLight first.

  • “Activate Night in AuraLight.” · “Turn AuraLight on.”
  • VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type & Reduce Motion options
  • Universal — iPhone & iPad
Check your controller

One app. Several controller families.

AuraLight includes Bluetooth adapters for ELK/MELK, Triones, SP110E and compatible Govee BLE controllers. Support depends on the exact model and firmware; not every model has been tested on physical hardware.

It does not control every LED strip. Wi-Fi-only, infrared-only and unsupported Bluetooth controllers are outside this release. Govee support targets basic power, color and brightness. Individual strip control requires a controller that supports separate zones.

Data Not Collected

On-device controls. No account.

No account. No analytics. No ads. No tracking. Lighting controls and audio analysis work on your device without an internet service. Your scenes and preferences use local app storage; microphone audio is analyzed in memory and is never saved or uploaded. The privacy policy explains system backups, website visits and optional support email.
