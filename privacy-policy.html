Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

AuraLight is a companion app for controlling compatible Bluetooth LED lighting controllers in your vehicle or around your space. The offline release does not collect, upload, sell or share personal data. There is no account, analytics, advertising or tracking in this release.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Bluetooth — used to discover nearby devices, connect to your selected LED controller and send it color, brightness and effect commands. Discovery may display nearby device names. This information is not uploaded. iOS may allow an existing controller connection or reconnection in the background; background behavior depends on the system.
  • Microphone (only while you use a microphone-based music-reactive effect) — audio from the microphone is analyzed in real time, entirely on your device, to measure loudness and beats so the lights can react to your music. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Only anonymous numeric levels (e.g., loudness, beat count) are derived, and they exist only in memory while the effect is running.
  • Music you play in-app (only when you choose a track on the Music screen) — when you pick an audio file to play through AuraLight, the app reads that file solely to play it and drive the lights from its sound. The file and its audio stay on your device — nothing is recorded, copied off your device, or uploaded. AuraLight only accesses the specific file you choose.
  • Motion sensors (only while you use the experimental “Warp Drive” driving-dynamics effect) — accelerometer/gyroscope data is processed in real time, on your device, to react to acceleration, braking, and cornering. Motion data is never recorded, saved, or transmitted.
  • Siri & Shortcuts (only if you choose to use them) — AuraLight receives lighting actions through Apple's system features. AuraLight does not upload these actions. Apple's handling of voice requests and other system data is governed by your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policies.

What is stored on your device (only)

The app saves your preferences and setup locally on your device so it works the way you left it: your saved color scenes, the remembered controller profile, strip layout, theme, and effect settings. This information stays in the app's private storage on your device and is never uploaded. Deleting the app removes it.

AuraLight writes technical diagnostic logs, including Bluetooth connection events and controller identifiers, to the device's standard logging system. AuraLight does not upload these logs. If you choose to share a diagnostic report for support, review its contents before sending it.

Data collection summary (Apple “Privacy Nutrition Label”)

Data Not Collected. AuraLight collects no data. Nothing is linked to your identity, and nothing is used for tracking.

Networking

AuraLight makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs, and no third-party data-collecting frameworks.

Website and support

This policy's on-device claims describe the app. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; those services receive ordinary web requests, such as your IP address and browser information. If you email support, the message and contact information you provide are used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or Apple account credentials.

Children's privacy

AuraLight does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.
