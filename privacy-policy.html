Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

AuraLight is a free, ad-supported app for controlling compatible Bluetooth LED lighting controllers in your vehicle or around your space. Your lighting settings and audio analysis stay on your device. Google AdMob provides banner ads and collects advertising-related data as described below. AuraLight does not require an account.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Bluetooth — used to discover nearby devices, connect to your selected LED controller and send it color, brightness and effect commands. Discovery may display nearby device names. This information is not uploaded. iOS may allow an existing controller connection or reconnection in the background; background behavior depends on the system.
  • Microphone (only while you use a microphone-based music-reactive effect) — audio from the microphone is analyzed in real time, entirely on your device, to measure loudness and beats so the lights can react to your music. Audio is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Only anonymous numeric levels (e.g., loudness, beat count) are derived, and they exist only in memory while the effect is running.
  • Music you play in-app (only when you choose a track on the Music screen) — when you pick an audio file to play through AuraLight, the app reads that file solely to play it and drive the lights from its sound. The file and its audio stay on your device — nothing is recorded, copied off your device, or uploaded. AuraLight only accesses the specific file you choose.
  • Siri & Shortcuts (only if you choose to use them) — AuraLight receives lighting actions through Apple's system features. AuraLight does not upload these actions. Apple's handling of voice requests and other system data is governed by your Apple settings and Apple's privacy policies.

What is stored on your device (only)

The app saves your preferences and setup in its private storage on your device: saved color scenes, the remembered controller profile, strip layout, theme, and effect settings. AuraLight does not send this information to developer-operated servers. Your device's system backups or transfers may include app data, depending on your Apple settings; the developer has no access to those backups.

AuraLight writes technical diagnostic logs, including Bluetooth connection events and controller identifiers, to the device's standard logging system. AuraLight does not upload these logs. If you choose to share a diagnostic report for support, review its contents before sending it.

Advertising and consent

Small, muted banner ads can appear on the connection and Setup screens. Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform handle ad delivery and consent. They may collect an IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, product interactions, crash information, performance information and other diagnostics. These data support advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, service operation and analytics. AuraLight does not send your LED settings, controller identifiers, microphone audio or selected music files to these services.

Google's consent messages appear where required. The app checks consent before starting the advertising SDK or requesting ads. Where iOS allows a tracking request, you can accept or decline Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. With permission and applicable consent, Google may use identifiers to personalize and measure advertising across apps and websites. If you decline tracking, the app requests non-personalized ads with restricted data processing; ads may be unavailable. Lighting features remain available regardless of your choice. The app does not serve ads when iOS reports tracking as restricted.

Advertising data may be linked to device identifiers, and authorized identifiers may be used for tracking. Google and participating advertising services process and retain their data according to their policies. See Google's privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps, and Google's mobile ads data disclosure. AuraLight does not operate a server storing your lighting or advertising records.

Your privacy choices and deletion

You can revoke Bluetooth or microphone permission for AuraLight in iOS Settings. Bluetooth permission is needed to control real lights; the demo remains available without it. Microphone permission is needed only for microphone-based effects; static controls and audio-file playback do not require it.

Use Ad privacy choices beside the Privacy Policy and Support links when that option is required for your region. You can revisit applicable consent and opt-out choices there. Change tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The app rechecks that permission on returning to the foreground. Google's privacy tools and the links above explain access and deletion choices for data Google holds; deleting AuraLight does not itself delete records already held by Google.

Local preferences and saved scenes remain until you change or delete them, or delete the app. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. Manage any system backups separately in your Apple settings. Device diagnostic logs are retained and removed by the operating system.

Data collection summary (Apple “Privacy Nutrition Label”)

The ad-supported release discloses advertising-related identifiers, usage data, approximate location and diagnostics. Some advertising data is linked to device identifiers; tracking requires permission. Local lighting scenes, microphone audio and music files are not collected. The App Store label covers the app and its included advertising services.

Networking

AuraLight's lighting controls and audio analysis work without an internet service. Advertising and consent messages require network access. This release has no account or cloud-sync feature. Selecting Privacy Policy or Support opens a website in your browser; those web requests are described below.

Website and support

This policy's on-device claims describe the app. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; those services receive ordinary web requests, such as your IP address and browser information. See the GitHub privacy statement and Google privacy policy for their handling and retention of website requests.

If you email support, the message and contact information you choose to provide are used to respond and follow up on your request. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing tianjiahe11@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or Apple account credentials.

Children's privacy

AuraLight is a general-purpose lighting utility and is not directed to children under 13. Ads are requested with Google's general-audience content rating, and the app suppresses ads when iOS reports restricted tracking permission. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or advertising, contact us and consult Google's privacy controls above.
